# Difficulty levels (rubric used for generating and tagging questions)

All levels must be answerable from the in-class material only (slides in `text/slides_wNN.txt`, in-class demo in `text/demo_wNN.txt`, values confirmed in `verify/`). No outside knowledge. Exactly one defensible answer.

| Level | Thai name | What the student must do | Typical shape |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ง่าย | Recall one fact, definition, name or constant stated on one slide. One step. | "X คืออะไร", "ข้อใดถูกต้องเกี่ยวกับ X", one-operation arithmetic |
| 2 | ปานกลาง | Distinguish two related ideas or apply one formula once to given numbers. The teacher-hinted items live here. | compare A vs B, one calculation with 2-3 numbers, pick the right component |
| 3 | ยาก | Chain 2-3 steps, or choose the right method for a short scenario, or spot which statement is wrong among near-correct ones. | multi-step calculation, scenario "which design fits", trap options built from real misconceptions |
| 4 | ยากมาก | Combine ideas (inside the week or across weeks), reason about edge cases or "what breaks if", or do a calculation with several traps (decimals, powers of 10, units, which count to use). Still solvable from the slides by a student who understands them. | diagnose a flawed claim or pipeline, compare three methods under a constraint, derive a number that needs 3+ steps |

Never test things the teacher said are NOT on the exam: exact T5 log-bucket formula, FlashAttention online-softmax rescaling formula, the compression equations (pruning, distillation maths), MinHash/SHA-256 internals, anything from the labs.
Mixed default for a realistic paper: per week 2 easy, 4 medium, 3 hard, 1 very hard (for 10 MCQ).
